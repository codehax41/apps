Data & Account Deletion Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety policies, Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), and global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA, DPDPA), this page provides clear instructions on how Astro AI users can permanently delete their account and associated personal data.

PERMANENT & IRREVERSIBLE ACTION:
Account deletion permanently destroys your calculated birth charts (D1, D9, D10), natal ephemeris coordinates, voice interaction recordings, and AI oracle dialogue histories. This action cannot be undone.

Method 1: In-App Immediate Deletion (Recommended)

You can execute complete account deletion immediately directly within the mobile application:

  1. Open the Astro AI app on your Android or iOS device.
  2. Navigate to the Personal Profile screen (Screen 25) by tapping your avatar or profile icon in the navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Privacy & Security section.
  4. Tap Delete Entire Profile.
  5. Review the permanent deletion disclosure modal.
  6. Type the confirmation word DELETE into the security verification prompt.
  7. Tap Confirm Permanent Deletion. Your account will be immediately wiped, all session tokens revoked, and you will be returned to the Welcome screen.

Method 2: Web-Based Deletion Request (Without Reinstalling the App)

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your mobile device, you can submit a manual deletion request without needing to reinstall the app:

Send an email from the address associated with your Astro AI account to:

privacy@astroai.quantum

Please include the following details in your message:

  • Subject Line: Account Deletion Request
  • Registered Email: The exact email used to sign in (or your federated Apple/Google/Facebook email).
  • Statement: "I hereby request the permanent deletion of my Astro AI account and all associated personal and astrological data."

Our Data Protection Officer will verify the request and complete the deletion within 48 hours, followed by a written confirmation email.

The 15-Step Cryptographic Purge Process

When an account deletion is initiated, our backend executes an automated 15-step permanent wipe routine across all data stores:

  1. STEP 1 Disable account authentication and invalidate active session tokens.
  2. STEP 2 Delete user profile record, display name, and avatar references.
  3. STEP 3 Purge exact date of birth, time of birth, and geographic coordinates.
  4. STEP 4 Erase computed Vedic divisional charts (Kundali D1, Navamsa D9, Dasamsa D10).
  5. STEP 5 Delete all saved astrological readings and planetary transit caches.
  6. STEP 6 Clear all AI conversation dialogues, user queries, and prompt histories.
  7. STEP 7 Delete voice session recordings, audio buffers, and speech telemetry.
  8. STEP 8 Purge uploaded audio attachments and voice cache chunks.
  9. STEP 9 Delete custom preferences, notification schedules, and language choices.
  10. STEP 10 Detach payment gateway customer identities.
  11. STEP 11 Revoke active JWTs and OAuth refresh tokens across all devices.
  12. STEP 12 Sever federated identity links (Google, Apple, Facebook).
  13. STEP 13 Remove user-owned cloud object storage containers.
  14. STEP 14 Hard delete primary user record from database clusters.
  15. STEP 15 Emit an anonymous audit event (ACCOUNT_DELETION_SUCCESS) without retaining personally identifiable information (PII).

Data Retention Exceptions

In accordance with statutory legal requirements, financial transaction records (such as GST invoices and payment reference numbers for the ₹7 Trial Pass) are retained for 7 years solely to satisfy statutory accounting and taxation audits. These records contain no astrological ephemeris, voice, or conversation data.
