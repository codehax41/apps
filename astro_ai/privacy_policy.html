Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Astro AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy, personal data, and celestial ephemeris records of our users across both Android and iOS applications. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, protect, and delete your information under global data protection frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

Zero AI Training Guarantee: We do NOT sell your birth chart data, conversation transcripts, or voice recordings to data brokers, nor do we use your private astrological queries to train foundational machine learning models.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Identity Data: Name, email address, profile photo (if provided via federated Google, Apple, or Facebook authentication), user identifier, and preferred language/voice settings.
  • Birth & Ephemeris Data: Date of birth, exact time of birth, birth city, geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude), and preferred Ayanamsa (e.g., Lahiri / Chitra Paksha). This data is strictly utilized to calculate astronomical planetary positions (Kundali D1, Navamsa D9, Dasamsa D10).
  • Voice & Audio Streams: When you interact with Devi AI Oracle via speech, audio packets are streamed in real time to provide conversational voice synthesis. Transient audio buffers are processed only during the active voice dialogue.
  • Payment & Entitlement Records: For one-time purchases (such as the ₹7 1-Day Trial Pass) or subscriptions, transaction identifiers, purchase timestamps, and subscription validity periods are maintained. Raw credit card numbers or banking secrets are never stored on our servers; they are processed directly by certified gateways (Razorpay, Google Play Billing, Apple In-App Purchase).
  • Device & Telemetry Data: App version, operating system version (Android/iOS), 3D graphics capability (Vulkan/Impeller/Metal), and anonymized crash analytics to maintain rendering stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To compute accurate astronomical ephemeris charts and astrological divisional Vargas.
  • To stream interactive voice and text responses from the Devi AI 3D Oracle.
  • To enforce authoritative subscription quotas, including the hard 30-minute voice allowance on the 1-Day ₹7 Pass.
  • To remember your multi-language preferences (Hindi, Indian English, Spanish, etc.).
  • To comply with statutory legal and tax obligations.

4. Data Retention Schedule

  • Ephemeris & Natal Profile: Stored securely while your account remains active.
  • Voice & Dialogue Transcripts: Automatically purged on a 90-day rolling time-to-live (TTL) window.
  • Account Deletion: When you trigger a profile purge, all personal data, charts, and dialogues are permanently deleted within 48 hours across our infrastructure.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Under applicable privacy laws, you possess fundamental rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You may download a sanitized JSON copy of your complete birth profile and chart data directly within the app (Profile $\rightarrow$ Export My Data).
  • Right to Rectification: You can modify your birth time, date, or place at any time in the profile settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): You may permanently purge your account and all associated records directly inside the app (Profile $\rightarrow$ Delete Entire Profile) or by submitting a web request following our Data Deletion Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Astro AI is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 18 for paid purchases). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered an account without parental consent, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

7. Security Measures

All client-server communications are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Sensitive birth records and session tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 with isolated key management.
