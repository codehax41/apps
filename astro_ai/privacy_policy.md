# Astro AI — Privacy Policy
**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026 • **Application:** AstroAI OS v5.0 (Android & iOS)

Astro AI ("we," "our," or "us") respects your personal data, celestial ephemeris inputs, and voice recordings. This Privacy Policy details our data practices across our mobile and cloud systems.

---

### Zero AI Training Guarantee
We do **NOT** sell your birth chart data, conversation transcripts, or voice recordings to third parties or data brokers. We do **NOT** use your private astrological queries to train foundational machine learning models.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Data:** Name, email, profile photo, federated auth identity (Google, Apple, Facebook, Email), language/voice preferences.
- **Birth & Ephemeris Data:** Date of birth, exact time of birth, birth city, geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude), and preferred Ayanamsa (Lahiri / Chitra Paksha). Used strictly to compute Vedic astronomical charts (Kundali D1, Navamsa D9, Dasamsa D10).
- **Voice & Audio Telemetry:** Live audio streams processed during interactive voice sessions with the Devi AI Oracle. Transient audio chunks are processed in real time and discarded following transcription.
- **Billing & Subscriptions:** Transaction IDs, timestamps, entitlement statuses (e.g. ₹7 1-Day Trial Pass with 30-minute voice cap). Raw payment credentials are processed securely by Razorpay, Google Play Billing, or Apple In-App Purchase and never stored on our servers.
- **Device & Graphics Telemetry:** OS version (Android/iOS), 3D graphics hardware capability (Vulkan/Impeller/Metal), and anonymized crash telemetry.

---

## 2. Sub-Processors & Infrastructure
- **OpenAI:** Realtime speech-to-speech and text reasoning API (zero training data retention agreement).
- **Sarvam AI:** Indic voice synthesis and STT engine for conversational Hindi and regional languages.
- **Google Cloud Platform & Firebase:** Secure user authentication, encrypted database storage, and push notifications.
- **Apple Inc. / Google LLC:** StoreKit and Google Play in-app purchase validation.

---

## 3. Data Retention Schedule
- **Natal Chart Data:** Maintained while user account is active.
- **Conversation Logs:** 90-day rolling time-to-live (TTL) expiration.
- **Account Deletion:** Immediate upon user request; complete irreversible purge executed within 48 hours.

---

## 4. User Rights & Data Deletion
- **Export Data:** Users can export a complete, sanitized JSON copy of all personal records inside the app (`Screen 25: Personal Profile -> Export My Data`).
- **Permanent Purge:** Users can permanently delete their account and all associated ephemeris records inside the app (`Screen 25: Personal Profile -> Delete Entire Profile`) or via our web deletion endpoint described in our [Data Deletion Policy](deletion_policy.md).
