1. Self-Service Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), users can permanently and irreversibly erase their account and all personal birth matrix information directly from the mobile app at any time.

How to Delete Your Data Inside the App:

  1. Open the Daivant AI Astrology app on your device.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Data & Privacy Management.
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap "Delete All Data & Account" and confirm the prompt.
